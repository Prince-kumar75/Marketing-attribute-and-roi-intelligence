## 1. Executive Summary

During FY 2025, our digital marketing operations deployed **\$4,678,276.51** across six primary marketing channels, generating **\$52,091,563.43** in gross revenue, yielding **\$47,413,286.92** in net marketing contribution profit, an overall portfolio **ROAS of 11.13x**, and an **ROI of 1,013.48%**.

While aggregate performance appears stellar, our end-to-end data audit revealed critical strategic misallocations and systemic reporting biases:

1. **Systemic Last-Touch Attribution Distortion:**  
   Under legacy Last-Touch attribution, **Google Ads** is credited with **34.02%** (\$188.89k) of conversion revenue, while **Facebook Ads** and **Instagram Ads** are credited with only **12.62%** and **10.31%**. However, our multi-touch journey analysis reveals that Facebook and Instagram are responsible for **48.85%** of all converting customer journey first-touches. Relying on Last-Touch models risks severely defunding the top of the funnel, which would subsequently starve Google Ads of downstream converters.
2. **High-Margin Owned Media & Organic Dominance:**  
   **Organic Search** (\$20.86M revenue, 49.33x ROAS) and **Email Marketing** (\$12.11M revenue, 45.13x ROAS) generated **63.28%** of total revenue while consuming just **14.77%** (\$691.1k) of total marketing spend.
3. **Paid Media Inefficiencies & Budget Bleeders:**  
   Paid digital advertising (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads) absorbed **72.77%** (\$3.40M) of the marketing budget. Ten specific campaigns consistently generated sub-1.5x ROAS with CAC exceeding \$75.00, consuming over \$320,000 in inefficient spend.
4. **Q4 Holiday Seasonality:**  
   Q4 (November–December) generated **38.4%** of annual revenue and net profit due to holiday shopping volume and elevated average order value (AOV).

## 4. Multi-Touch Attribution Analysis: Resolving the Last-Touch Fallacy

Traditional marketing analytics relies on single-touch models (First-Touch or Last-Touch), which assign 100% of conversion credit to one single touchpoint. In a modern omnichannel journey where customers interact across search, social ads, and email before converting, single-touch attribution introduces severe distortions.

### Key Strategic Findings from Attribution Modeling

1. **The Google Ads Mirage:**  
   Google Ads appears to produce 34.02% of all company revenue under Last-Touch attribution because prospects search brand keywords immediately before making a purchase. However, under First-Touch, Google Ads only initiates 9.94% of customer journeys. Google Ads is largely **capturing existing intent** created by social discovery and organic content rather than creating it.
2. **The Facebook & Instagram Discovery Engine:**  
   Under Last-Touch, Facebook Ads looks mediocre (12.62% share). However, under First-Touch, Facebook Ads generates **28.19%** of all customer acquisitions, and Instagram Ads generates **20.66%**. Together, Meta platforms originate **48.85%** of all paying customer journeys.
3. **The Position-Based (40-40-20) Gold Standard:**  
   Position-Based attribution awards 40% to the journey introducer (first touch), 40% to the converter (last touch), and 20% to middle nurture steps. Under Position-Based modeling, Organic Search (26.61%), Google Ads (21.17%), and Facebook Ads (20.17%) achieve an equitable balance reflecting their true economic value.

## 9. Conclusion

The data confirms that the marketing department is a highly profitable growth engine. However, by retiring legacy Last-Touch reporting in favor of **Position-Based Attribution**, cutting spend on underperforming paid campaigns, and reinvesting into scalable owned/search channels, executive leadership can drive an estimated **\$10.1M in incremental revenue** and **\$10.1M in net profit** with zero additional budget increase.
